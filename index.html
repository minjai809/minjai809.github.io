Pursuing live VFX work for performances, he coldly accepts the imbalance between artificial and natural light, weaving it into his work itself. If society is like illuminating narrow alleys at night, he lights to conceal what should remain unseen. Yet in his art, one glimpses the faint outlines of boundaries that cannot be fully hidden. His artistic philosophy, playing with light without offering solutions, remains unformed.
