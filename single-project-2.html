Our planet - Shiba Yukio

Our planet depicts the vast distances from the solar system to the edge of the universe and the unimaginable 5 billion years until Earth's demise, using the metaphor of an ordinary girl named ‘Earth’. Through dialogue and rap that playfully utilize the nuances of language, it wittily captures our seemingly repetitive yet ever-changing daily lives.

The setting is Cosmos Apartment Complex, Block 19. The family of ‘Earth,’ a girl who had been alone for 600 million years, moved into Cosmos Apartments today. Next door lives ‘Moon,’ a girl slightly smaller than Earth. The two became inseparable best friends, always together. But due to their orbits, the Moon, who had grown close, gradually drifted apart. Now, they are childhood friends who have parted ways and can no longer meet.
